Summary of the multimedia material
======================================================================

This attachment accompanies the manuscript "PushAround: Collaborative Path
Clearing via Physics-Informed Hybrid Search" together with the author response
and diff file. It contains two objects.

1) video.mp4
   A single video showing (i) the simulation of the proposed planner clearing a
   corridor for a large external vehicle in two scenarios, including the
   W-clearance connectivity graph overlays during execution, and (ii) the
   real-world execution of the same framework with two Mecanum-wheel robots
   pushing cardboard obstacles in a 5x6 m workspace.

   Usage: play with any MP4 player.
   Value: it shows, in a form that static figures cannot, how the successive
   planned pushes progressively enlarge the reachable W-clearance region until
   the start and the goal become connected, and how closed-loop replanning
   recovers from contact, force and slippage deviations in the hardware runs.

2) evaluation_data/
   The per-trial records behind every number reported in the revised manuscript
   and in the author response, together with the resolved configuration of each
   study:

   * density_scaling/            Obstacle-density sweep: 5 policies x 7
                                 densities x 10 seeds, with success status,
                                 planning and execution time, push count,
                                 simulation count and failure reason per trial.
   * physics_mismatch/           Planner-execution mismatch study: nominal
                                 planning with execution-side perturbations of
                                 obstacle mass, lateral friction, spinning
                                 friction and robot force limit.
   * robot_scaling_modefix/      Robot-team scaling (2, 3, 4 robots) on the
     robot_scaling/              current code revision, and the superseded
                                 pre-fix revision for reference.
   * robot_scaling_baselines_postfix/
                                 The same robot-team study for the two baselines
                                 on the current revision, including
                                 diagnostics.csv, which counts how often the
                                 shared mode-generation rescue paths
                                 (backtracking contact assignment and temporary
                                 per-task robot release) were triggered.
   * wccg_cpp_paper_300/         WCCG connectivity validation against a
                                 continuous and a rasterized configuration-space
                                 reference on 300 scenes: agreement counts,
                                 false positives/negatives and query timings.
   * baselines_paper_ready/      Machine-readable digest of the baseline
                                 comparisons for the mismatch and robot-scaling
                                 studies.

   Usage: open the .csv files with any spreadsheet program. The mapping file
   evaluation_data/README.md links every record to the corresponding table or
   figure of the manuscript and of the response letter. Two scripts regenerate
   the figures from these records: evaluation_data/make_figures.py reproduces
   every figure of the response letter, and
   evaluation_data/plot_density_scaling_paper.py reproduces the three-panel
   obstacle-density figure of the paper.
   Value: it makes the reported results independently checkable. The exact trial
   counts, seeds, success and failure composition, planning and execution times
   and replan/push counts of every condition are provided, so that the claims of
   the paper, including the robustness margins under physics mismatch and the
   statistical confidence intervals, can be verified without re-running the
   simulator.
