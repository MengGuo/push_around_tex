Supplementary material for "PushAround: Collaborative Path Clearing via
Physics-Informed Hybrid Search", RA-L manuscript 26-3602.

1. PushAround.mp4
   One video illustrates simulated corridor clearing, hardware execution with
   two robots, execution-side physical-parameter mismatch, and scaling to teams
   of two, three and four robots. Play it with an MP4-capable media player.
   It complements the paper by showing obstacle motion, evolving connectivity
   and robot coordination over time. The final version updates the cover.

2. evaluation_data/
   CSV/JSON records, configurations and plotting scripts support the WCCG
   validation and the density, team-size and mismatch studies. The README maps
   records to the final manuscript and explains the legacy plotting outputs.
   Inspect the records or run the Python scripts to examine these results
   without rerunning the simulator.
   This package does not contain the per-trial records of the separate early
   Scenario-1 comparison/ablation study (Table II), the hardware study, or the
   complete planner implementation. It is not a complete experimental archive.
